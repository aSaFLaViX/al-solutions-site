Pick a camera frame and run the analysis. The system finds the pallets and cartons, spots damage, cross-checks what it sees against the written safety policy, and sends a person only the things it is genuinely unsure about. Every frame here is drawn, not photographed.

What is actually happening

1

Detection with confidence

The model boxes every object in the frame and scores it. Nothing is detected silently: what the system saw, you see too.

2

Checked against a written rule

What appears in the frame is cross-checked against your own policies. A five-pallet stack is not just an image, it is a breach of a specific clause.

3

Human in the loop

Poor light or occlusion lowers confidence, and the system says so instead of guessing. A person decides, and the decision is recorded.
